💡 What did we discover?

  • Magnetic force is a non-contact force — it acts at a distance, and the pull grows weaker as the distance grows.
  • Only magnetic materials respond: iron, steel, nickel and cobalt. Most metals — copper, aluminium, gold — do not!
  • Every magnet has two poles, North and South.
  • Like poles repel, unlike poles attract.
  • A compass needle is a tiny magnet that turns to line up with the magnetic field.
