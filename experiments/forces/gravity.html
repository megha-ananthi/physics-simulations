💡 What did we discover?

  • Gravity is a non-contact force — it acts between ANY two objects with mass, without touching, even across empty space.
  • More mass = stronger pull. More distance = weaker pull. That is why you feel the Earth's gravity but not your friend's!
  • Weight = mass × g, and g changes on other worlds — on the Moon it is about 1/6th of Earth's, so you would weigh less there.
  • In a vacuum all objects fall together — it is air resistance, not weight, that makes feathers and paper drift down slowly.
  • Orbiting = perpetual free fall — satellites and astronauts are falling around the Earth all the time, moving sideways so fast they keep missing it.
🚀 Chandrayaan orbits the Moon 🌊 Moon's pull makes ocean tides 🍎 Newton's falling apple ⚖️ Your mass is the same on the Moon — your weight is not!
