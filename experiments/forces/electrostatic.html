🎈 Electrostatic Force

Drag the balloon onto the sweater and rub it side to side — then bring it near the target!

💡 What did we discover?

  • Rubbing transfers charge — electrons move from the sweater onto the balloon. Charge is transferred, never created: the sweater's + marks always match the balloon's − marks.
  • Like charges repel, unlike charges attract — two balloons rubbed on the same sweater push each other away.
  • A charged object attracts even NEUTRAL objects — it shifts the charges inside them (induction): paper bits jump up, a wall holds the balloon and a water stream bends.
  • Electrostatic force is a non-contact force — it acts at a distance, no touching needed.
  • Dry air keeps the charge, humid air leaks it away — that is why static tricks work best in dry weather.
