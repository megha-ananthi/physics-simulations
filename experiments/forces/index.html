💪 Types of Forces

Push, pull, attract, repel — discover the forces that move our world!

🤔 What is a Force?

A force is a push or a pull. Some forces need to touch an object to act on it. Others work like magic from a distance — no touching needed!

🤝 Contact Forces Must touch the object: muscular force (kicking, pulling) and friction (rubbing surfaces).
🪄 Non-Contact Forces Act from a distance: gravitational, magnetic and electrostatic forces.
