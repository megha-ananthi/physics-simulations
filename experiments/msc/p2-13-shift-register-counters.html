SHIFT REGISTER, RING COUNTER AND JOHNSON COUNTER

Aim

To construct and verify the operation of a 4-bit shift register, a ring counter and a Johnson (twisted ring) counter using D flip-flops.

Apparatus required

IC 7474 (dual D flip-flop) × 2, digital IC trainer kit, patch cords.

Theory

Shift register: a chain of D flip-flops with a common clock, in which the output Q of each flip-flop is connected to the D input of the next. On every clock pulse the data moves one stage along. Data entered one bit at a time at D1 can be taken out serially at Q4 (serial-in serial-out) or all at once from Q1–Q4 (serial-in parallel-out).

Ring counter: a shift register whose last output Q4 is fed back to the first input D1. A pattern loaded with the preset and clear inputs circulates as long as clock pulses are applied; with four flip-flops the pattern repeats after every four clock pulses (mod-4). It is self-decoding — no extra decoder is needed to tell which state it is in.

Johnson (twisted ring) counter: the inverted output Q̄4 of the last flip-flop is fed back to D1. An n-stage Johnson counter gives 2n different states, so four flip-flops give eight states (mod-8). Its advantage is that it needs only n flip-flops to produce 2n states.

Logic diagrams

Pin diagram of IC 7474

Procedure

  1. Connect the circuit as shown in the logic diagram, using two IC 7474s (each contains two D flip-flops). Give +5 V to pin 14 and ground to pin 7 of each IC.
  2. Connect the clock inputs (pins 3 and 11) of all four flip-flops together to the pulser (clock) of the trainer kit.
  3. Keep the PR̄ and CLR̄ inputs at logic 1 during normal operation.
  4. Shift register: clear all flip-flops with CLR̄, apply the serial data at D1, give clock pulses one at a time and note Q1–Q4 after each pulse.
  5. Ring counter: connect Q4 to D1. Load the starting pattern with PR̄ / CLR̄, then apply clock pulses and note the outputs.
  6. Johnson counter: connect Q̄4 to D1, clear (or preset) the flip-flops, apply clock pulses and note the outputs.
  7. Verify the truth tables.

Result

The 4-bit shift register, ring counter and Johnson counter were constructed using D flip-flops and their truth tables were verified. The ring counter circulates the loaded pattern with 4 states (mod-4) and the Johnson counter goes through 2n = 8 states (mod-8).

Precautions

  1. Make the connections according to the IC pin diagram and check VCC (pin 14) and ground (pin 7) before switching on.
  2. Use a debounced pulser for the clock so that each press gives exactly one pulse.
  3. Keep PR̄ and CLR̄ at logic 1 while counting; they are active-low.
