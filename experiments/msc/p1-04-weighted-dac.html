⚖️ D/A Converter — 4-bit Binary Weighted Resistor Method

🏠 Lab

Each input bit sends a current into the op-amp through its own resistor. The resistors are binary weighted (50 k, 100 k, 200 k, 400 k), so the MSB sends 8 parts of current, the next 4, then 2, then 1. The IC 741 adds the currents and turns them into a voltage that climbs in equal steps.

D/A CONVERTER — 4-BIT BINARY WEIGHTED RESISTOR METHOD

Aim

To construct a 4-bit D/A converter using operational amplifier by binary weighted resistor method.

Apparatus and components

IC 741, resistors (50 kΩ, 100 kΩ, 200 kΩ, 400 kΩ), potentiometer for Rf, dual regulated power supply, 1–0 toggle switches, multimeter, etc.

Circuit diagram

Formula

Vo = − Vref [ D3/R4 + D2/R3 + D1/R2 + D0/R1 ] Rf

where Vref = 5 V, D3 D2 D1 D0 represent 0 or 1 depending on the binary combination, R4 = 50 kΩ, R3 = 2R4 = 100 kΩ, R2 = 4R4 = 200 kΩ, R1 = 8R4 = 400 kΩ, and Rf is the feedback resistance.

Procedure

The digital to analog converter (D/A converter) converts the digital input into its equivalent analog voltage. The digital input is represented by D3 D2 D1 D0 with D3 as MSB and D0 as LSB. The MSB bit D3 is connected to the inverting input through a resistor R4. D2 is connected through a resistor R3 = 2R4, D1 through a resistor R2 = 4R4 and D0 through a resistor R1 = 8R4. Therefore the arrangement is called a binary weighted resistor D/A converter.

  1. The connections are made as shown in the figure.
  2. The full scale output voltage (FSV) is fixed by varying the feedback resistance. For convenience the FSV is adjusted to 1.5 V; then for the digital input 0000 the analog output will be 0 V, for 0001 it will be 0.1 V, for 0010 it will be 0.2 V, etc.
  3. The digital inputs are changed from 0000 to 1111 step by step and the analog output voltage is measured at the output terminal of IC 741.
  4. The FSV can also be fixed as 5 V and the output measured for the different input combinations.

Result

A 4-bit binary weighted resistor D/A converter is constructed using operational amplifier.

Precautions

  1. Use close-tolerance resistors whose values are exactly in the ratio 1 : 2 : 4 : 8.
  2. Connect ±12 V to pins 7 and 4 of IC 741 before applying the inputs.
  3. Set the full-scale voltage with Rf at input 1111 before taking the readings.
