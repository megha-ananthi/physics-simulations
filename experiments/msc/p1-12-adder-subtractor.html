➕ 4-bit Binary Adder & Subtractor (IC 7483)

🏠 Lab

IC 7483 is four full adders in a chain: each stage adds two bits and a carry and passes its carry on to the next. To subtract, the same adder adds the complement of B (1's complement with an end-around carry, or 2's complement with Cin = 1), and a BCD adder adds 0110 whenever the binary sum goes past 9.

4-BIT BINARY ADDER AND SUBTRACTOR USING IC 7483

Aim

To design and implement a 4-bit binary adder and subtractor using IC 7483.

Apparatus required

Theory

4-bit binary adder. A binary adder produces the arithmetic sum of two binary numbers. It is built from full adders connected in cascade: the output carry of each full adder is connected to the input carry of the next. The augend bits A and the addend bits B are numbered from right to left, subscript 0 being the LSB. The input carry C0 enters the LSB stage and ripples through to the output carry C4. IC 7483 is a 4-bit binary full adder: it adds A3A2A1A0, B3B2B1B0 and C0 (pin 13) and gives the sum S3S2S1S0 and the carry C4 (pin 14). For every stage:

Si = Ai ⊕ Bi ⊕ Ci
Ci+1 = AiBi + Ci(Ai ⊕ Bi)

4-bit binary subtractor. A − B is found by adding the complement of B to A, so inverters (IC 7404) are placed between each B input and the adder. (i) 1's complement method: the adder forms A + B̅; the output carry is added back to the LSB (end-around carry — pin 14 joined to pin 13). If C4 = 1 the result S is positive; if C4 = 0 the result is negative and its magnitude is the 1's complement of S. (ii) 2's complement method: the input carry C0 is made 1, so the adder forms A + B̅ + 1 = A + (2's complement of B). If C4 = 1 the carry is ignored and S is the positive result; if C4 = 0 the result is negative and its magnitude is the 2's complement of S.

4-bit adder/subtractor. Addition and subtraction are combined in one circuit with one 7483 and four XOR gates (IC 7486). The mode input M goes to one input of every XOR gate and to C0. When M = 0, B ⊕ 0 = B and C0 = 0: the circuit adds. When M = 1, B ⊕ 1 = B̅ and C0 = 1: it subtracts (2's complement).

BCD adder. Two BCD digits (each ≤ 9) and an input carry give a sum of at most 19. The digits are first added in the upper 4-bit adder to give the binary sum. A binary sum from 1010 to 1111, or one that produces a carry, is not a valid BCD digit, so 6 (0110) must be added to it. From the K-map the correction signal is

Y = C4 + S3(S2 + S1)

Y drives the B2 and B1 inputs of the lower 7483 (B3 = B0 = 0), which therefore adds 0110 when Y = 1; its own carry is ignored. Y is also the output carry (tens digit) of the BCD adder.

Notation: the bits are numbered A3…A0, B3…B0, S3…S0 (subscript 0 = LSB) as in the 7483 connection diagram. The manual numbers the same bits A4…A1, B4…B1, S4…S1, so its correction term Y = S4(S3 + S2) is the same as S3(S2 + S1) here.

Procedure

  1. Check the ICs and fix IC 7483 (and IC 7404 / IC 7486 when needed) on the trainer kit.
  2. Connect VCC (+5 V) to pin 5 and ground to pin 12 of IC 7483 (pin 14 and pin 7 for IC 7404, IC 7486, IC 7408 and IC 7432).
  3. Adder: connect A3–A0 to pins 1, 3, 8, 10 and B3–B0 to pins 16, 4, 7, 11 through the logic switches; ground C0 (pin 13); connect S3–S0 (pins 15, 2, 6, 9) and C4 (pin 14) to LEDs.
  4. Subtractor (1's complement): pass B3–B0 through four NOT gates of IC 7404 and join pin 14 (C4) to pin 13 (C0).
  5. Subtractor (2's complement): pass B through the NOT gates and connect pin 13 to VCC (C0 = 1); C4 shows the sign (0 = negative).
  6. Adder/subtractor: pass each B bit through an XOR gate of IC 7486 whose other input is the mode line M; connect M also to pin 13. M = 0 adds, M = 1 subtracts.
  7. BCD adder: wire the two 7483s and the correction logic (IC 7408, IC 7432) as in the diagram; apply only BCD digits (0000–1001).
  8. Apply the inputs listed in the observation tables, note the LED outputs and verify them with the truth tables.

Precautions

  1. VCC and GND of IC 7483 are pins 5 and 12 — not the corner pins as in most ICs.
  2. Switch off the supply while making or changing connections.
  3. Do not leave unused inputs floating: a floating TTL input behaves as logic 1.
  4. Apply only valid BCD digits (0–9) to the BCD adder.
  5. Check the patch cords and LEDs for continuity before starting.
