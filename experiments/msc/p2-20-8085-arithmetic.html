➗ 8085 — 8-bit Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication & Division

🏠 Lab

The 8085 can only add and subtract directly. Subtraction is done by adding the 2’s complement, multiplication by adding the multiplicand again and again, and division by subtracting the divisor again and again and counting how many times it fits.

How it works

A = second number (8051H), B = first number (8050H) — ADD B adds them column by column.

The 8085 subtracts by adding the 2’s complement: A − B = A + (2’s complement of B).
