🔤 8085 — Code Conversion

🏠 Lab

The same number can be written in different codes. The 8085 converts decimal ↔ hex by counting in two registers at once (one kept in decimal by DAA), and hex ↔ ASCII by splitting a byte into its two hex digits and adding or subtracting 30H (plus 07H for the letters A–F).
