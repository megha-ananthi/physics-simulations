🦶 Pressure = Force ÷ Area
🏠 Lab

The same push can feel gentle or sharp. It depends on the area it is spread over. Pressure = Force ÷ Area: a small area makes a big pressure, and a big area makes a small one.

💡 What did we discover?

  • Pressure is the force acting on each unit of area. Pressure = Force ÷ Area, measured in N/m² = pascal (Pa).
  • Same force, smaller area ⇒ bigger pressure. That is why a nail is pointed, a knife is sharp and high heels sink into sand.
  • Same force, bigger area ⇒ smaller pressure. That is why bags have broad straps, buckets have broad handles, and people place a round cloth under a load on their head.
  • Lying down on soft sand spreads your weight over a large area, so you sink less than when you stand.
🎒 Broad bag straps 🪣 Broad bucket handle 🧺 Cloth ring under a head load 🔩 Pointed nails 🔪 Sharp knives 🚜 Wide tractor tyres on soft soil
