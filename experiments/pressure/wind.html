💡 What did we discover?

  • Wind is air moving from a region of high pressure to a region of low pressure. When the pressures become equal, the air stops flowing.
  • The bigger the pressure difference, the faster the wind. A harder-blown balloon pushes air through the straw faster.
  • Sea breeze (day): land heats up faster than water. Warm air over the land rises and leaves low pressure, so air from the sea (higher pressure) blows towards the land.
  • Land breeze (night): land cools faster and the sea stays warmer. Low pressure forms over the sea, so the wind blows from the land to the sea.
  • High-speed winds come with reduced air pressure. That is why balloons swing together when you blow between them, a paper strip rises when you blow over it, and storm winds can lift light roofs.
  • During storms, open doors and windows and holes in banners let air pass through, so the pressure difference stays small.
🎣 Fishermen sail out at night with the land breeze and come back with the sea breeze 🪁 Kites fly high on the beach in the afternoon 🌴 Coastal trees bent towards the land 🌀 Cyclone season: keep doors and windows open, tie down tin roofs 🪧 Flex banners and hoardings with holes 🚲 Punctured tyre: air rushes out from high to low pressure
