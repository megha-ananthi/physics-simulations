💡 What did we discover?

  • Liquids exert pressure. A column of water pushes on the bottom of its container — the balloons bulge.
  • Pressure depends on the height of the liquid column, not on the amount of liquid. Equal heights give equal bulges even when one pipe holds much more water.
  • The deeper the point, the larger the pressure. Holes lower down squirt faster and farther; the ground-floor tap gets the strongest stream.
  • Liquids push in all directions — on the sides of a container too. Holes at the same height spurt out equally all around.
  • Overhead tanks are placed high to increase the pressure at the taps. A dam is broad at the base because the push of water is largest near the bottom.
  • Connected vessels fill to the same level, whatever their shapes.
